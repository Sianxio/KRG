Lords of Chaos

A Wizard's Duel

Two sorcerers meet in a moonlit vale. Only one will walk away. Summon your host, hurl your fire, and strike down the rival wizard.

Tap a unit to select it, then tap a glowing tile to move — or tap a foe beside it to strike.
📖 Spells opens your grimoire: bolts, fire, healing and summoned beasts. Casting costs mana ⬡ and action points.
⌛ End Turn passes to the enemy wizard. Mana returns each turn.
Slay the enemy wizard to win. Guard your own with your life.
